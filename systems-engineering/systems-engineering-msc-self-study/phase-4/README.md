# Phase 4 — Agile, digital, mission, and process-centered engineering

These courses are retained as distinct subjects. They follow the core lifecycle and quantitative phases so that agile delivery, digital threads, digital twins, mission engineering, and process redesign are grounded in actual requirements, architecture, integration, test, and analytic-model experience.

### Recommended order

1. [**EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering**](en-645-780-agile-systems-engineering.md)
2. [**EN.645.782 — Foundations of Digital and Mission Engineering**](en-645-782-foundations-of-digital-and-mission-engineering.md)
3. [**EN.645.783 — Systems Engineering Process Improvement**](en-645-783-systems-engineering-process-improvement.md)

Agile Systems Engineering and Digital and Mission Engineering can be swapped. Process Improvement should remain last because it asks the learner to assess and redesign processes they should first have experienced.
